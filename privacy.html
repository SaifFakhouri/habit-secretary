1. Overview

Habit Secretary is a personal accountability and habit tracking application built and maintained by me, a solo developer. This Privacy Policy explains what information I collect, why I collect it, and how you can control it.

By using Habit Secretary, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

Short version: I collect only what's necessary to run the app and provide AI coaching. I do not sell your personal data to anyone, ever.

2. Information I Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, I collect:

  • Email address
  • User ID (system-generated)
  • Authentication method (password or magic link)

Profile & Onboarding Data

During onboarding and through app usage, I collect:

  • Your name or display name
  • Personal goals and goal descriptions
  • "Perfect day" template content you define
  • Preferred morning briefing time
  • Quiet hours preferences
  • Timezone and locale information

Habit & Activity Data

  • Habit definitions and categories you create
  • Habit completion logs and timestamps
  • Weekly review data and insights
  • Streaks and progress records

AI Conversation Data

The AI secretary feature involves:

  • Messages you send to the AI secretary
  • AI-generated responses
  • Memory context summaries generated from your conversation history
  • Morning briefing content

This content is used to provide continuity and personalization in your AI coaching experience.

Notification Preferences

  • Whether you have granted notification permissions
  • Your configured reminder schedules
  • Notification delivery and interaction records

Device & Technical Data

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash reports and diagnostic data (for stability purposes)
  • Session tokens stored locally on your device

What I Do Not Collect

  • I do not collect your precise location
  • I do not access your contacts, camera, or microphone
  • I do not collect payment information directly (handled by Apple/Google)
  • I do not build advertising profiles

3. How I Use Your Information

I use the data I collect to:

  • Provide the app: authenticate your account, store your habits, and deliver AI coaching responses
  • Personalize AI responses: conversation history and memory context are used to make the AI secretary relevant and contextual to your goals
  • Send notifications: morning briefings, habit reminders, and check-ins based on your configured schedule and quiet hours
  • Generate insights: weekly review summaries and progress analysis
  • Improve reliability: crash monitoring and diagnostic data help me fix bugs and maintain stability
  • Communicate with you: transactional emails (account verification, password reset) — no marketing emails without your explicit opt-in

I do not use your data for behavioral advertising, data brokering, or any purpose beyond operating and improving Habit Secretary.

4. Third-Party Services

Habit Secretary relies on the following third-party services to function:

Supabase

My backend infrastructure. Supabase stores your account data, habit logs, messages, and app state. Supabase is SOC 2 compliant. See their Privacy Policy.

Google Gemini

Powers the AI secretary functionality. Your messages and relevant context are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate responses. Google's use of this data is governed by their Privacy Policy and applicable API terms. I do not permit Google to use your data to train their models under my API agreement.

Expo / React Native

The app is built with Expo. Expo may collect limited technical data for push notification delivery. See Expo's Privacy Policy.

Apple / Google

App distribution, in-app purchases (if applicable), and push notification infrastructure are handled by Apple (iOS) and Google (Android), subject to their respective privacy policies.

Crash Monitoring

I use a crash monitoring service to receive diagnostic reports when the app crashes. These reports contain device metadata and stack traces — not personal content from your habits or conversations.

I do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data Retention

I retain your data for as long as your account is active. Specifically:

  • Account data: retained until you delete your account
  • Habit logs and messages: retained while your account exists; you can clear AI memory at any time from within the app
  • Crash/diagnostic data: typically retained for 90 days
  • After account deletion: I delete your personal data within 30 days of account deletion, except where retention is required by law

6. Your Rights & Controls

You have meaningful control over your data inside the app:

Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data directly from within the app (Settings → Account → Delete Account). This removes your profile, habits, messages, and all stored data.

AI Memory Clear

You can clear the AI secretary's memory context at any time from the app settings. This resets the contextual memory used to personalize responses without deleting your account.

Notification Control

You can modify or disable notifications at any time within the app or through your device's system settings.

Data Access & Portability

You may request a copy of the personal data I hold about you by contacting me at the address below.

Residents of Specific Regions

If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, California, or another jurisdiction with specific privacy rights, you may have additional rights including the right to access, rectify, or restrict processing of your data. Contact me to exercise these rights.

7. Security

I take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Supabase row-level security for database access control
  • Secure local storage for session tokens on-device
  • Regular dependency updates and security monitoring

No system is completely secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact me immediately.

8. Children's Privacy

Habit Secretary is not directed at children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If I become aware that I have collected data from a child without parental consent, I will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When I do, I will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. For material changes, I will notify you within the app. Continued use of Habit Secretary after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
